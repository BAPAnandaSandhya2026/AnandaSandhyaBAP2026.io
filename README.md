## About Ananda Sandhya

Ananda Sandhya is one of many annual cultural events held by the Bengali Association of Pittsburgh. The 2026 celebration brings together the community of all ages through music, dance, drama, and storytelling.

This year, we explored the theme **"The Bioscope & Its Influencers,"** celebrating influential figures in Bengali films, including **Uttam Kumar, Satyajit Ray, and Rituparno Ghosh**.

The website was created to preserve the event while making information easily accessible to performers, attendees, families, and the community.

## Design & Development

The website was designed with the idea of combining traditional Bengali culture with a modern digital experience.

The design emphasizes:

* Cultural identity
* Elegant typography
* Visual storytelling
* Easy navigation
* Responsive layouts
* Interactive content
* Preservation of event memories

Special attention was given to creating a website that could function not only as an event information page, but also as a digital archive of Ananda Sandhya 2026.
